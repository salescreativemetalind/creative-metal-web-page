## How to use this file
1. Go to https://search.google.com/search-console (property: creativemetalind.com)
2. Paste one URL into the "Inspect any URL" bar at the top, press Enter
3. Click **Request Indexing**
4. Repeat for the next URL

**Note:** Google allows only ~10-12 manual "Request Indexing" per day per
property, and only ONE URL at a time. So work through this list top-to-bottom
over 4-5 days. Also submit the sitemap once (see bottom of file).

## IMPORTANT: Submit the sitemap (do this once, on Day 1)

Instead of relying only on manual requests, submit your sitemap so Google
finds all pages automatically:

1. In GSC, left sidebar -> **Sitemaps**
2. Enter: `sitemap-index.xml`
3. Click **Submit**

This covers all 48 URLs in one action and is the most reliable way to get
the full site re-crawled.
