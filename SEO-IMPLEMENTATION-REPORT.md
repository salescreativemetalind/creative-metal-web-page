# SEO Implementation Report

## Executive Summary

The site had overlapping supplier intent across the homepage and two target pages. The Vadodara landing page presented itself primarily as an SS pipe supplier and contained extensive pipe-only material, while `/ss-pipe-supplier` was framed as a generic guide and included competitor references and unsupported price/supply claims. A second existing pipe-supplier guide also had a “Top/Leading Supplier” title and H1 despite informational content.

The existing URLs and design were retained. The Vadodara page now leads with local, broad stainless-steel supply; `/ss-pipe-supplier` now presents a commercial pipe enquiry; and the separate guide page is retargeted to informational buyer intent. Homepage metadata and its local stainless-steel section were broadened, and contextual links now connect the pages. Unsupported ranking, stock, lead-time and delivery superlatives were removed from the areas edited. No ranking outcome is guaranteed.

**Keywords/topics covered:** stainless steel supplier in Vadodara, stainless steel products, SS 304/316L and other grades listed on the site, pipes, tubes, plates, sheets, fittings and flanges, Vadodara location and quotation enquiry.

**Technical SEO changes:** Local supplier title, description, H1, Open Graph and Twitter metadata; self-referencing canonical retained; index/follow directive retained.

**Remaining issues:** The detailed grade/size/standard table is based on existing site content and has not been independently verified with the business. Confirm its current accuracy before treating it as a firm availability statement.

## Homepage Cannibalization

**Before:** The homepage title and description led with “SS Pipe Supplier Vadodara”; its H1 emphasized pipe/fitting/flange supply, and a separate section claimed “#1 SS Pipe Supplier in Vadodara.” The separate `/top-stainless-steel-pipe-supplier-india-quality-assured` guide also used a supplier-focused title and H1. These overlapped the target pages.

**Changes:** The homepage title, description and social metadata now describe Creative Metal Industries as a broad industrial metal supplier. Its H1 remains broad and Vadodara-oriented. The local stainless-steel homepage section now introduces the product range and links separately to the Vadodara supplier page and the SS pipe supplier page. Unverified homepage claims about stock quantities, same-day delivery and “15+ years” were removed from that section.

**Intended page mapping:**
- Homepage: Creative Metal Industries / broad industrial metal supplier.
- `/stainless-steel-supplier-vadodara`: local stainless-steel supplier and product forms.
- `/ss-pipe-supplier`: commercial SS pipe supplier and quote intent.
- `/top-stainless-steel-pipe-supplier-india-quality-assured`: informational buying guide about pipe types and supplier checks.
