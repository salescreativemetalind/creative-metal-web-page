## Why Choose Hastelloy C-276?

Hastelloy C-276 is often called the "universal" corrosion-resistant alloy because it handles both oxidizing AND reducing environments:

**1. Resists Virtually All Chemicals**
Unlike most alloys that resist either oxidizing OR reducing acids, C-276 handles both. It withstands sulfuric acid (all concentrations up to 70°C), hydrochloric acid (all concentrations up to 50°C), phosphoric acid, acetic acid, formic acid, and chlorine gas.

**2. Outstanding Pitting & Crevice Corrosion Resistance**
With PREN (Pitting Resistance Equivalent Number) of ~70, Hastelloy C-276 is virtually immune to pitting in chloride environments. Compare: SS 316L PREN = 25, Duplex 2205 PREN = 35.

**3. Excellent Weldability**
Unlike some nickel alloys, C-276 can be welded without post-weld heat treatment. The low carbon content (0.01% max) prevents carbide precipitation in the weld heat-affected zone.

**4. Stress Corrosion Cracking Immunity**
C-276 is immune to chloride stress corrosion cracking (SCC) — a failure mechanism that causes sudden catastrophic pipe rupture in austenitic stainless steels.

**Q: What is the difference between Hastelloy C-276 and Inconel 625?**
A: Both are nickel-based. C-276 has more molybdenum (16% vs 9%) giving it better resistance to reducing acids (HCl, H₂SO₄). Inconel 625 has niobium for better strength and fatigue resistance. C-276 is better for pure corrosion service; 625 is better when you need corrosion + strength + high temperature.

A: Yes. C-276 resists sulfuric acid at all concentrations up to 70°C, and at dilute concentrations up to boiling point. For concentrated hot sulfuric acid above 70°C, consult our engineers for specific grade recommendation.
