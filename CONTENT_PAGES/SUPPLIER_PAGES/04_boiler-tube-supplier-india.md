## IBR Certification for Boiler Tubes

**What is IBR?**
Indian Boiler Regulations (IBR) is a statutory requirement for all materials used in boilers and pressure vessels operating above 1 kg/cm² pressure in India. The regulation mandates that tubes/pipes used in boilers must be certified per IBR guidelines.

**IBR Documentation We Provide:**
- ✅ IBR Form III-C (Material Certificate from approved manufacturer)
- ✅ Chemical composition test report
- ✅ Mechanical test report (Tensile, Flattening, Flaring, Hardness)

**Q: What is the difference between SA 179 and SA 192 boiler tubes?**
A: SA 179 is a minimum wall tube (cold-drawn) for heat exchangers and economizers. SA 192 is a minimum wall tube (hot-finished) for pressure-service boiler tubes. SA 192 has slightly better yield strength and is the standard specification for water tube boiler applications.

**Q: Do you supply IBR-certified boiler tubes?**
A: Yes. All our boiler tubes are sourced from IBR-approved mills and come with genuine IBR Form III-C documentation. This is mandatory for all boiler applications in India and we never supply without proper IBR certification.

**Q: What is SA 213 T91 tube used for?**
A: SA 213 T91 (9Cr-1Mo-V) is used for superheater and reheater tubes in supercritical and ultra-supercritical power plants. It handles temperatures up to 650°C with excellent creep strength, allowing higher operating efficiency.

**Q: How to select the right boiler tube grade?**
A: Selection depends on operating temperature and pressure. Below 425°C: SA 179/192. Up to 570°C: SA 213 T11. Up to 600°C: SA 213 T22. Up to 650°C: SA 213 T91. Our engineers can help with specific grade selection for your boiler.
