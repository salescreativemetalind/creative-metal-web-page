**Q: What is the difference between ASTM A249 and A269?**
A: Both cover austenitic SS tubes. A249 is specifically for boiler, superheater, and heat exchanger tubes (higher testing requirements — hydro test mandatory). A269 is for general corrosion-resistant service (hydro test can be substituted by eddy current). For heat exchangers, A249 is the correct specification.

**Q: What tube OD and gauge should I specify?**
A: Most common: 19.05mm OD x 16 BWG (1.65mm wall) or 25.4mm OD x 14 BWG (2.11mm wall). The engineer designs based on heat duty, pressure, and fouling. If you have an existing exchanger, match the existing tube specification exactly.

**Q: Do you supply U-bend tubes?**
A: Yes. We supply straight tubes and can arrange U-bending to your specified radius. U-bend minimum radius depends on tube OD and material — typically 1.5x to 3x tube OD for stainless steel.

**Q: What is the delivery time for heat exchanger tubes?**

**Q: Can you supply tubes for re-tubing of existing heat exchangers?**
