## Why Monel 400? Key Advantages

**1. Outstanding Seawater Resistance**
Monel 400 is virtually immune to seawater corrosion, making it the preferred material for marine and naval applications. It resists flowing seawater up to velocities of 4.5 m/s without erosion-corrosion.

**2. Excellent HF Acid Resistance**
Monel 400 is one of the few alloys that can handle hydrofluoric (HF) acid at all concentrations and temperatures. This makes it essential for alkylation units in refineries.

**3. High Strength**
With tensile strength of 480 MPa and good ductility, Monel 400 offers better mechanical properties than most corrosion-resistant alloys. It retains strength well at sub-zero temperatures too.

**4. Good Fabricability**
Monel 400 can be hot-worked, cold-worked, welded (with proper filler), and machined — making it versatile for fabrication of complex piping systems.

**Q: What is the difference between Monel 400 and Inconel 625?**
A: Monel 400 is a nickel-copper alloy (63% Ni, 30% Cu) — best for seawater and HF acid. Inconel 625 is a nickel-chromium-molybdenum alloy (58% Ni, 22% Cr, 9% Mo) — best for extreme temperatures + corrosion. Inconel 625 costs 30-40% more than Monel 400.

**Q: Can Monel 400 be welded?**
A: Yes. Monel 400 has good weldability using GTAW (TIG) with matching filler wire ERNiCu-7. Preheat is not required. Post-weld heat treatment is typically not needed for corrosion service. PWHT at 870°C may be specified for stress relief in critical applications.
