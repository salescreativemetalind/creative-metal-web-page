## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is SS 304 round bar price per kg?**
A: SS 304 bright round bar (6-25mm): ₹270-320/kg. SS 304 black bar (25-100mm): ₹240-280/kg. Prices vary by diameter and finish. Call for specific size quotation.

**Q: What is the difference between SS round bar and SS rod?**
A: Generally they refer to the same product. "Round bar" typically means solid circular cross-section in larger diameters (10mm+). "Rod" often refers to smaller diameters (under 12mm) or wire rod in coils. Both are ASTM A276.

**Q: Do you supply SS round bar with test certificate?**
A: Yes. Every round bar comes with Mill Test Certificate showing chemical composition and mechanical properties per ASTM A276/A479. PMI testing can be arranged on request.

**Q: What is the tolerance on SS bright round bar?**
A: Bright drawn bars: H9 tolerance (e.g., 20mm = 20.000 to 19.948mm). Centreless ground bars: H7 tolerance (e.g., 20mm = 20.000 to 19.979mm). Black bars: typically -0/+1mm.

**Q: Do you supply SS flat bar and hex bar also?**
A: Yes. We supply SS flat bars (3mm to 50mm thick, 10mm to 200mm width) and hexagonal bars (10mm to 75mm across flats) in SS 304, 316L, and other grades.

**Q: What is the minimum order quantity?**
A: No minimum order. We sell even single bars. However, per-bar pricing is slightly higher than bulk (100 kg+) orders. For best rates, order 500 kg or more.
