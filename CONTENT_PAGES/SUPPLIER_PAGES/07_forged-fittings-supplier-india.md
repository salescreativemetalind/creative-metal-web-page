## Pressure Class Explained — ASME B16.11

| Class | Pipe Schedule Rating | Max Pressure (at ambient) | Typical Use |
|-------|---------------------|--------------------------|-------------|
| 2000# | SCH 80 (Threaded) | ~138 bar | General industrial |
| 3000# | SCH 80 (SW) / SCH 160 (THD) | ~207 bar | Standard high-pressure |
| 6000# | SCH 160 (SW) / XXS (THD) | ~414 bar | Very high pressure |
| 9000# | XXS (SW only) | ~621 bar | Ultra-high pressure |

**Most Common:** Class 3000# socket weld fittings are the industry standard for small-bore high-pressure piping (instrumentation, sampling, drains, vents).

## Socket Weld vs Threaded — When to Use Which

| Factor | Socket Weld (SW) | Threaded (THD/NPT) |
|--------|-----------------|-------------------|
| Connection | Welded (fillet weld) | Screwed (no welding) |
| Pressure Rating | Higher (3000#-9000#) | Lower (2000#-6000#) |
| Leak Resistance | Excellent (welded seal) | Good (thread sealant) |
| Maintenance | Permanent (cut to remove) | Easy removal/replacement |
| Best For | Permanent high-pressure | Instrumentation, drains, vents |
| Sizes | 1/8" to 4" | 1/8" to 4" |
| Fire Safety | Better (welded) | Risk of burn-through at threads |
| Typical Use | Process piping, HP lines | Utility, instrument, temporary |

**General Rule:** Use socket weld for permanent process piping (especially hydrocarbons and high pressure). Use threaded for instrument connections, drain/vent points, and lines that need periodic disconnection.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is forged fittings price in India?**
A: SS 304 forged fittings (3000# class): ₹80-1200 per piece depending on type and size. Carbon steel (A105): ₹35-380 per piece. Pricing is per-piece, not per-kg, for forged fittings.

**Q: What is the difference between forged fittings and buttweld fittings?**
A: Forged fittings are for small-bore piping (1/8" to 4") using socket weld or threaded connections. Buttweld fittings are for larger piping (1/2" to 48") using butt weld connections. Forged fittings handle higher pressures in small sizes.

**Q: What does 3000# class mean?**
A: 3000# (3000 pound) is a pressure rating class per ASME B16.11. It means the fitting is designed to match SCH 80 pipe for socket weld or SCH 160 pipe for threaded connections. It can handle approximately 207 bar pressure at ambient temperature.

**Q: Socket weld or threaded — which is better?**
A: Socket weld is better for permanent, high-pressure, hazardous service (welded seal). Threaded is better when you need to disconnect periodically (instruments, drains). For hydrocarbon service above Class 2000, most codes require socket weld over threaded.
