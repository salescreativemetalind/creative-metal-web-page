## API 5L PSL1 vs PSL2 — Complete Comparison Guide

The main difference between API 5L PSL1 and PSL2 is the level of quality control and testing. PSL2 (Product Specification Level 2) has stricter chemical composition limits, mandatory impact testing (Charpy), mandatory non-destructive examination (NDE), and tighter mechanical property ranges compared to PSL1 (Product Specification Level 1). PSL2 is required for critical pipeline service (sour gas, offshore, high-pressure transmission) while PSL1 is adequate for general pipeline and structural use.

## What is API 5L?

API 5L is the international standard published by the American Petroleum Institute covering seamless and welded steel line pipes for oil and gas transportation systems. It defines pipe grades (strength levels), dimensional tolerances, testing requirements, and manufacturing procedures for pipes used in pipeline construction worldwide.

**API 5L is used for:**
- Oil transmission pipelines
- Natural gas transmission and distribution
- Water injection lines in oil fields
- Offshore flowlines and risers
- Process plant piping (select applications)
- Slurry pipelines

## Detailed Differences — PSL1 vs PSL2

### 1. Chemical Composition

**PSL1:** Only specifies maximum limits for elements. No minimum limits for most elements. No Carbon Equivalent (CE) requirement.

**PSL2:** Specifies both maximum AND minimum limits. Mandatory Carbon Equivalent (CE_IIW ≤ 0.43 or CE_Pcm as applicable) to ensure weldability in the field.

| Element | PSL1 X52 (max) | PSL2 X52 (max) | PSL2 Added Control |
|---------|---------------|----------------|-------------------|
| Carbon | 0.28% | 0.22% | Tighter limit |
| Manganese | 1.40% | 1.40% | Same |
| Phosphorus | 0.030% | 0.025% | Tighter limit |
| Sulfur | 0.030% | 0.015% | Much tighter |
| CE (IIW) | Not required | ≤ 0.43 | Mandatory |
| CE (Pcm) | Not required | ≤ 0.25 | Mandatory |

**Why it matters:** Lower carbon and sulfur in PSL2 means cleaner steel with better weldability, toughness, and resistance to hydrogen-induced cracking (HIC).

### 2. Mechanical Testing

**PSL1:** Tensile test required. No mandatory Charpy impact test. No yield-to-tensile ratio limit.

**PSL2:** Tensile test with tighter acceptance range. Mandatory Charpy impact test at specified temperature. Yield-to-tensile ratio ≤ 0.93. Mandatory hardness testing.

| Test | PSL1 | PSL2 |
|------|------|------|
| Tensile | Required | Required (tighter range) |
| Charpy (CVN) | Not mandatory | Mandatory (min energy specified) |
| Test Temperature | N/A | Design temperature (e.g., -20°C, -40°C) |
| Y/T Ratio | Not specified | ≤ 0.93 |
| Hardness | Not mandatory | Mandatory (≤ 250 HV10) |
| Flattening/Bend | Required | Required |
| Guided Bend (weld) | Required for welded | Required |

### 3. Non-Destructive Examination (NDE)

**PSL1:** NDE not mandatory for seamless pipe. For welded pipe, only weld seam inspection required.

**PSL2:** Full NDE mandatory for ALL pipe types:
- Ultrasonic inspection of pipe body (for laminations)
- Ultrasonic or radiographic inspection of weld seam
- Electromagnetic inspection of pipe ends
- Surface inspection (visual + magnetic/liquid penetrant)

### 4. Traceability & Marking

**PSL1:** Traceability to heat (melt) level. Marking as per standard requirements.

**PSL2:** Individual pipe traceability required. Each pipe must be traceable to its specific heat, lot, and test results. Enhanced marking with additional information.

### 5. Supplementary Requirements (SR)

PSL2 pipe often requires additional Supplementary Requirements:

| SR Code | Requirement | Typical For |
|---------|-------------|-------------|
| SR1 | Fracture Toughness (CVN) | Cold climate pipelines |
| SR2 | Drop Weight Tear Test (DWTT) | Fracture propagation control |
| SR3 | HIC Testing (NACE TM0284) | Sour gas service |
| SR4 | SSC Testing (NACE TM0177) | Sour gas service |
| SR5 | CVN on HAZ | Weld quality assurance |
| SR6 | Hardness of HAZ ≤ 250 HV | Sour service |
| SR15 | CTOD Testing | Offshore/deep water |

## When to Specify PSL1

PSL1 is adequate for:
- Low-pressure gas distribution (city gas, LPG)
- Water pipelines (non-critical)
- Structural applications (piling, casing)
- Process piping where ASME B31.3 allows
- Agricultural water lines
- Non-sour, onshore, low-risk service
- Budget-constrained projects where code allows

## When to Specify PSL2

PSL2 is required for:
- High-pressure gas transmission (trunk pipelines)
- Sour service (H₂S present — NACE MR0175)
- Offshore pipelines and risers
- Deep-water flowlines
- Cold climate (-20°C and below)
- Pipeline in populated areas (consequence of failure)
- Where DNV-OS-F101 or CSA Z662 applies
- All projects where safety is critical

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is the difference between PSL1 and PSL2?**
A: PSL2 has stricter chemistry (lower carbon, sulfur), mandatory impact testing, mandatory NDE, hardness limits, and individual pipe traceability. PSL1 has basic requirements suitable for non-critical service. PSL2 costs 15-20% more.

**Q: Can I use PSL1 pipe for high-pressure gas pipeline?**
A: Most pipeline codes (ASME B31.8, IS 17285) require PSL2 for high-pressure gas transmission. PSL1 is typically limited to low-pressure distribution (below 20 bar). Check your applicable code.

**Q: Is PSL2 mandatory for sour service (H₂S)?**
A: Yes. Sour service per NACE MR0175/ISO 15156 requires PSL2 with supplementary requirements SR3 (HIC test) and SR4 (SSC test). PSL1 is NOT acceptable for sour gas pipelines.

**Q: What does API 5L X52 mean?**
A: "X52" indicates the minimum yield strength in ksi (52,000 psi = 360 MPa). Higher grade numbers mean stronger pipe allowing thinner walls or higher pressure. The ISO designation for X52 is L360.

**Q: Do you supply API 5L PSL2 pipe?**
A: Yes. We supply API 5L PSL2 seamless and welded pipe in grades X42 through X65 with full documentation including Charpy test reports, NDE certificates, and coating reports. Call for availability and pricing.

**Q: What is Carbon Equivalent (CE) in API 5L PSL2?**
A: CE is a formula that predicts weldability. CE_IIW = C + Mn/6 + (Cr+Mo+V)/5 + (Ni+Cu)/15. PSL2 limits CE to ≤ 0.43 ensuring the pipe can be field-welded without preheating in most conditions.
