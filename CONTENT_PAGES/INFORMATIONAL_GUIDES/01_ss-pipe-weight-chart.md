## SS Pipe Weight Chart — Complete Reference (Per Metre)

This comprehensive SS pipe weight chart covers all standard sizes from 1/2" to 24" NB across all schedules (5S, 10S, 40, 80, 160, XXS). Weights are calculated for stainless steel (density 7.93 g/cm³). For carbon steel, multiply by 7.85/7.93 = 0.99 (essentially the same). Use this chart for material estimation, transport planning, and support design.

## Weight Formula

**Pipe weight per metre (kg/m) = (OD - Wall) × Wall × 0.02491**

Where:
- OD = Outside Diameter in mm
- Wall = Wall Thickness in mm
- 0.02491 = constant for stainless steel (density 7.93 g/cm³)

For carbon steel: constant = 0.02466 (density 7.85 g/cm³)

## How to Use This Weight Chart

**1. Material Estimation:**
Calculate total pipe weight for procurement: Weight/metre × total length required.
Example: 100 metres of 2" SCH 40 SS 304 pipe = 100 × 5.44 = 544 kg

**2. Cost Calculation:**
Total cost = Weight (kg) × Price per kg
Example: 544 kg × ₹290/kg = ₹1,57,760

**3. Transport Planning:**
- Standard truck capacity: 10-15 tonnes
- 6-metre pipe bundle weights help determine truck loading
- Example: One bundle of 2" SCH 40 (typically 19 pipes) = 19 × 6m × 5.44 kg/m = 620 kg

**4. Support Design:**
Pipe support spacing and load calculations require pipe weight + content weight + insulation weight:
- Pipe weight: from chart above
- Water-filled weight: add water density × ID² × π/4
- Insulation weight: depends on type and thickness

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How do I calculate SS pipe weight per metre?**
A: Use formula: Weight (kg/m) = (OD - Wall) × Wall × 0.02491. Or refer to the charts above for standard sizes.

**Q: What is the weight of 2" SCH 40 SS pipe per metre?**
A: 5.44 kg per metre (for SS 304/316L). Per foot: 1.66 kg. Per 6-metre length: 32.64 kg.

**Q: Is SS pipe weight same as MS pipe weight?**
A: Nearly identical. SS density is 7.93 g/cm³ vs MS at 7.85 g/cm³ — only 1% difference. For practical purposes, you can use the same weight chart for both materials.

**Q: How many 2" SCH 40 pipes in 1 tonne?**
A: Weight per 6m length = 5.44 × 6 = 32.64 kg. Pipes per tonne = 1000/32.64 = approximately 30 pipes.

**Q: What is the weight of 4" SCH 40 SS pipe per 6 metre length?**
A: 16.07 kg/m × 6m = 96.42 kg per pipe (approximately 96-97 kg per 6-metre length).
