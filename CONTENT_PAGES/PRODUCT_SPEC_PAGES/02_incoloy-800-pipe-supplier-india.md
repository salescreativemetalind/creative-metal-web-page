## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is the difference between Incoloy 800, 800H, and 800HT?**
A: All three have similar base composition (30-35% Ni, 19-23% Cr). The difference is carbon and Al+Ti content which controls grain size and creep strength. 800HT has the most Al+Ti (0.85-1.20%) for best high-temperature creep. Use 800HT for services above 900°C.

**Q: What is Incoloy 800 pipe price in India?**
A: ₹1,800-3,000/kg depending on grade (800/800H/800HT) and size. 800HT is most expensive due to tighter chemistry control.

**Q: Is Incoloy 800 same as Inconel 800?**
A: "Incoloy" and "Inconel" are trade names of Special Metals Corporation. Incoloy 800 is iron-based (39% Fe, 32% Ni). Inconel alloys are nickel-based (50-72% Ni). They are different alloy families. The correct designation is Incoloy 800.

**Q: Where is Incoloy 800H used?**
A: Primarily in petrochemical reformer piping (steam methane reformers), ethylene furnace tubes, and any service requiring creep resistance at 800-1000°C. Also used in power plant superheaters above SS 321's limit.
