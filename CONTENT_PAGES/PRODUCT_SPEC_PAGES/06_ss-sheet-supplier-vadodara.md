## SS Sheet vs SS Plate — What's the Difference?

|-----------|-------|-------|

| Finish | 2B, BA, No.4, Mirror | No.1, 2B (for 6-8mm) |
| Surface Quality | Superior (smooth, uniform) | Rougher (scale removed) |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is SS 304 sheet price per kg in Vadodara?**
A: SS 304 sheet (2B finish, 0.5-2mm): ₹250-290/kg. SS 304 plate (6mm+): ₹230-270/kg. Thinner gauges cost more per kg due to additional cold rolling. Call for exact rate.

**Q: What is the difference between 2B and BA finish?**
A: 2B is the standard cold-rolled finish (slightly reflective, smooth). BA (Bright Annealed) is mirror-like reflective finish achieved by annealing in hydrogen atmosphere. BA costs 10-15% more and is used for decorative/reflective applications.

**Q: Do you supply SS sheet with PVC coating?**
A: Yes. Most decorative-grade sheets (2B, BA, No.4, Mirror) come with PVC protective film on both sides to prevent scratches during transport and fabrication. Film removed after installation.

**Q: What is minimum order for SS sheet?**
A: Single sheet — no minimum order. We sell by weight (kg) or by sheet. For cut pieces, minimum billing ₹3,000.

**Q: Do you supply SS 310 plate for furnace applications?**
A: Yes. We stock SS 310/310S plates in 3mm to 20mm for high-temperature furnace components. Service temperature up to 1050°C. Thicker sizes and larger quantities on 2-3 week order.

**Q: Can you supply perforated or checkered SS sheet?**
A: Perforated sheets (round/square holes) and checkered/diamond pattern sheets available in SS 304. These are typically made-to-order with 2-3 week lead time.
