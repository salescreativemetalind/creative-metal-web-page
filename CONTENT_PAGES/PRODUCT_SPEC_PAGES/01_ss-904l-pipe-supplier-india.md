## Why SS 904L? Key Advantages

**1. Sulfuric Acid Resistance:** 904L is one of the best stainless steels for sulfuric acid — handles all concentrations at moderate temperatures. SS 316L fails badly in H₂SO₄.

**2. Phosphoric Acid:** Excellent in phosphoric acid containing fluorides and chlorides. Used extensively in fertilizer (DAP/MAP) plants.

**3. Chloride Pitting Resistance:** PREN ~36 (vs 25 for 316L) means significantly better pitting resistance in warm chloride solutions.

**4. Cost-Effective vs Nickel Alloys:** At ₹800-1100/kg, 904L is 3-4x cheaper than Hastelloy C-276 (₹4000-5500/kg) while handling many of the same environments.

**5. Copper Addition:** The 1-2% copper gives 904L specific resistance to sulfuric acid that other austenitic grades lack.
