## How to Select Hardox Grade

| Application | Impact Level | Recommended Grade |
|-------------|-------------|-------------------|
| Dump truck body liner | High impact + sliding | Hardox 450 or HiTuf |
| Crusher liner | Extreme impact | Hardox 400 or HiTuf |
| Chute/hopper liner | Sliding abrasion | Hardox 450 or 500 |
| Conveyor skirt plate | Moderate sliding | Hardox 400 |
| Screen liner | Low impact, high abrasion | Hardox 500 |
| Bucket cutting edge | High impact + abrasion | Hardox 400 |
| Shredder hammer | Extreme impact | Hardox HiTuf |

**Rule of thumb:** Higher hardness = better abrasion resistance, but lower impact toughness. Use Hardox HiTuf or 400 where impact is severe. Use 500/550 where pure sliding abrasion dominates.
