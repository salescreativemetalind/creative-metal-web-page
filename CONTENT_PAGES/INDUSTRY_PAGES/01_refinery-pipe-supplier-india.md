## NACE Compliance for Sour Service

Many refinery units handle sour (H₂S-containing) streams requiring NACE MR0175/ISO 15156 compliance:

**NACE Requirements We Meet:**
- Material in solution-annealed or normalized condition
- Hardness ≤ 22 HRC (≤ 248 HV10) for carbon/low-alloy steel
- HIC testing per NACE TM0284 (for plates and pipes in sour service)
- SSC testing per NACE TM0177 (for specific applications)
- Complete NACE compliance documentation with material

**NACE-Compliant Materials in Stock:**
- CS pipe A106 Gr.B (normalized, hardness controlled)
- CS fittings A234 WPB (normalized and tempered)
- CS flanges A105N (normalized)
- SS 316L (solution annealed — inherently NACE compliant)
- Duplex 2205 (solution annealed, ferrite controlled)
- SA 516 Gr.70 NACE HIC tested plates
