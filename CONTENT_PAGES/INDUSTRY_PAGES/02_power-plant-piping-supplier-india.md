## P91 — The Critical Power Plant Material

P91 (9Cr-1Mo-V) is the most critical and expensive piping material in modern power plants:

**Why P91 is used:**
- Service temperature up to 650°C (vs P22 limit of 600°C)
- Higher creep strength allows thinner walls (less weight, less thermal stress)
- Enables supercritical and ultra-supercritical boiler designs
- Reduces pipe support loads and header weights

**P91 handling requirements:**
- Mandatory preheat (200-250°C minimum) before welding
- Mandatory PWHT (760°C ± 15°C for 2-4 hours) after welding
- Controlled cooling rate during PWHT
- Hardness verification after PWHT (200-270 HV)
- No cold work without subsequent heat treatment
- Storage in dry, covered area (corrosion-sensitive)
