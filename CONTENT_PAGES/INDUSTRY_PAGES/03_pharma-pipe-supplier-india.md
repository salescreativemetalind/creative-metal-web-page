## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What pipe grade is required for pharmaceutical applications?**
A: SS 316L is mandatory for all product-contact surfaces in pharma. The low carbon (≤ 0.03%) prevents sensitization during welding, and molybdenum provides corrosion resistance to cleaning chemicals. SS 304 can be used for non-product utility piping.

**Q: What surface finish is required for pharma piping?**
A: Ra ≤ 0.8 µm for general product-contact piping. Ra ≤ 0.6 µm for WFI and high-purity water. Ra ≤ 0.4 µm for parenteral/injectable manufacturing. Finish is measured using profilometer and documented in surface finish certificate.

**Q: Do you supply electropolished pipe?**
A: Yes. We supply SS 316L seamless pipes with internal electropolishing to Ra ≤ 0.8, ≤ 0.6, or ≤ 0.4 µm as required. Electropolishing is performed by specialized vendors and certified with Ra measurement reports.

**Q: What documentation do you provide for pharma?**
A: Complete package: MTC (3.1/3.2), surface finish report, passivation certificate, heat number traceability, chemical/mechanical test reports, and PMI report. All documentation designed to support GMP/cGMP audit requirements.

**Q: Can you supply sanitary (tri-clamp) fittings?**
A: Yes. We supply tri-clamp (TC) fittings, sanitary elbows, tees, and valves in SS 316L with electropolished finish per 3-A Sanitary Standards. Available for 1" to 4" tube sizes.

**Q: What is your delivery time for pharma-grade pipe?**
A: Standard SS 316L (2B finish): 2-5 days from stock. Electropolished (Ra ≤ 0.8): 1-2 weeks. High-purity (Ra ≤ 0.4): 2-3 weeks. We recommend ordering 3-4 weeks ahead for EP material.
