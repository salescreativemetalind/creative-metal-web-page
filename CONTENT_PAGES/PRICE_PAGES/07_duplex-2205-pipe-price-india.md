## Why is Duplex Steel So Expensive?

Duplex 2205 costs 2-3x more than SS 304 for several reasons:

**1. Complex Alloy Composition**
Duplex 2205 contains 22% chromium, 5% nickel, 3% molybdenum, and 0.15% nitrogen. The molybdenum alone (₹3,500/kg raw) adds significant cost. The nitrogen addition requires special steelmaking processes (AOD/VOD).

**2. Difficult Manufacturing**
Duplex steel's dual-phase microstructure (50% austenite + 50% ferrite) requires precise temperature control during hot working and solution annealing. Any deviation results in sigma phase or loss of corrosion resistance.

**3. Limited Producers**
Only a few mills worldwide produce duplex seamless pipes to reliable quality: Sandvik (Sweden), Tubacex (Spain), Salzgitter (Germany), and select Indian mills for welded. Limited supply supports higher pricing.

**4. Testing Requirements**
Every heat of duplex requires: ferrite count, impact testing at -46°C, corrosion testing (ASTM G48), and sometimes NACE compliance. These add substantial testing costs.

**5. Lower Demand Volume**
Compared to SS 304/316L (millions of tonnes/year), duplex production is a fraction, so economies of scale don't apply.

## When is Duplex 2205 Worth the Premium?

**Duplex 2205 is cost-effective when:**
- Chloride environment exceeds SS 316L capability (>100 ppm Cl⁻ at elevated temperature)
- Higher strength reduces pipe schedule (thinner wall = less weight = material savings)
- Long design life required (30+ years) in aggressive environments
- Total cost of ownership matters (less corrosion = less maintenance = less downtime)

**Duplex 2205 saves money vs exotic alloys when:**
- Environment is too aggressive for 316L but doesn't need Inconel/Hastelloy
- Temperature is below 250°C (above this, consider austenitic or nickel alloys)
- Pitting resistance (PREN 35) is sufficient for your conditions

**Q: Why is Duplex pipe so expensive compared to SS 304?**
A: Duplex contains expensive alloying elements (molybdenum, higher chromium), requires complex manufacturing with precise heat treatment, and is produced by fewer mills in lower volumes. It costs approximately 2.5x the price of SS 304.

**Q: Is Duplex 2205 better than SS 316L?**
A: For chloride corrosion resistance and strength, yes. Duplex 2205 has PREN 35 vs 316L's PREN 25, and double the yield strength. However, for temperatures above 250°C or in non-chloride environments, SS 316L may be sufficient and 50% cheaper.

**Q: What testing is done on Duplex 2205 pipe?**
A: Standard testing includes: hydrostatic test, ferrite count (30-70% per ASTM E562), Charpy impact test at -46°C, corrosion test (ASTM G48 Method A), intergranular corrosion test (ASTM A262), and full chemical/mechanical testing per MTC.

**Q: Can Duplex 2205 be welded?**
A: Yes, but with precautions. Use matching filler (ER2209), control heat input (0.5-2.5 kJ/mm), maintain interpass temperature below 150°C, and avoid PWHT unless specified. Incorrect welding can destroy the duplex microstructure.
