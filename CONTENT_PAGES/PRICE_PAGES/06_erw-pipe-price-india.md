## Factors Affecting ERW Pipe Price

**1. Steel Grade & Class:** Heavy grade (Class C) costs more than Light grade (Class A) due to thicker walls and more material per metre.

**2. Coating:** Black (plain) is cheapest. Galvanized (GI) adds 15-20%. Epoxy/powder coated adds 25-35%.

**3. Brand/Mill:** APL Apollo, Tata Tubes, Surya Roshni, Maharashtra Seamless are premium brands. Local mills offer 5-10% lower pricing.

**4. Size:** Smaller sizes (1/2" to 1") have higher per-kg cost than larger sizes (4" to 6") due to rolling economics.

**5. End Finish:** Plain ends are standard. Threaded and coupled ends add ₹5-8/kg for the threading operation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is ERW pipe price per kg in India today?**
A: MS ERW pipe (IS 1239 Medium) costs ₹58-80/kg depending on size. GI ERW pipe costs ₹69-92/kg. SS 304 ERW pipe costs ₹190-250/kg. July 2026 rates. Call for exact quote.

**Q: What is the difference between ERW pipe price in Light, Medium, and Heavy grade?**
A: Light grade is cheapest (thinnest wall). Heavy grade costs 8-12% more than Light grade of the same size due to more material (thicker wall). Medium grade falls in between and is the most commonly specified.

**Q: Is ERW pipe cheaper than GI pipe?**
A: Yes. ERW (black) pipe is 15-20% cheaper than GI pipe. The difference is the zinc galvanizing coating on GI pipe which prevents rusting. Use GI for exposed/outdoor applications and plain ERW for concealed/indoor use.

**Q: What is IS 1239 ERW pipe?**
A: IS 1239 is the Indian Standard specification for mild steel ERW pipes used for water, gas, and steam service. It covers three grades: Light (A), Medium (B), and Heavy (C). Medium grade is most commonly used for general plumbing and industrial applications.

**Q: Can ERW pipe be used for gas lines?**
A: For low-pressure gas distribution (under 7 bar), IS 1239 Heavy grade ERW pipe is acceptable. For high-pressure gas (CNG, industrial gas), seamless pipe per IS 1239 or ASTM A106 is required.
