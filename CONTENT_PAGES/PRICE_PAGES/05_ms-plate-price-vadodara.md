## Factors Affecting MS Plate Price in Vadodara

**1. Thickness Range**
Thinner plates (6-8mm) cost ₹2-5/kg more than thick plates (25-50mm) because they require more rolling passes and have higher finishing costs.

**2. Grade & Specification**
Standard IS 2062 E250A is the cheapest. Higher grades (E350, SA 516, SAILMA) cost more due to alloying elements and additional testing requirements.

**3. Source Mill**
- SAIL (Bhilai, Bokaro, Rourkela): Most competitive pricing
- ESSAR/AM-NS: Good quality, moderate premium
- JSW: Consistent quality, slightly premium
- Imported (Korea, China, Ukraine): Varies with exchange rate and duties

**4. Plate Size**
Standard sizes (1500x6300, 2000x6300) are stock items. Custom cut or non-standard sizes carry 5-10% cutting premium.

**5. Quantity & Payment**
Full truck load (20+ tonnes) saves ₹1-2/kg over small lot purchase. Advance payment gives 1-2% additional discount.

**6. Testing & Certification**
Plates with TC (Test Certificate) from mill are standard. Third-party tested or UT (Ultrasonic Testing) inspected plates cost 5-8% more.
