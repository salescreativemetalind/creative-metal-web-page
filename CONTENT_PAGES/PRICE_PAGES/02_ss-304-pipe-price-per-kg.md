## What is SS 304 Stainless Steel?

SS 304 (also called 18/8 stainless steel) is the most widely used austenitic stainless steel grade in the world. It contains approximately 18% chromium and 8% nickel, giving it excellent corrosion resistance and good mechanical properties for a wide range of industrial applications.

**Chemical Composition of SS 304:**

| Element | Percentage |
|---------|-----------|
| Chromium (Cr) | 18.0 - 20.0% |
| Nickel (Ni) | 8.0 - 10.5% |
| Carbon (C) | 0.08% max |
| Manganese (Mn) | 2.0% max |
| Silicon (Si) | 1.0% max |
| Iron (Fe) | Balance |

**Key Properties:**
- Tensile Strength: 515 MPa minimum
- Yield Strength: 205 MPa minimum
- Density: 7.93 g/cm³
- Max Service Temperature: 870°C (intermittent), 925°C (continuous)

## Factors Affecting SS 304 Pipe Price

**1. Nickel Price on LME**
SS 304 contains 8-10% nickel. When LME nickel moves by $500/tonne, SS 304 pipe price changes by approximately ₹8-12/kg domestically. This is the single biggest price driver.

**2. Seamless vs Welded**
SS 304 seamless pipe costs 35-50% more than welded. For a 2" pipe: seamless = ₹290/kg vs welded = ₹210/kg. Choose based on pressure requirements.

**3. Indian Mill vs Import**
- Indian mills (Jindal, Salem Steel, Ratnamani): ₹270-310/kg
- Imported (Sandvik, Tubacex, NKK): ₹350-420/kg
- Difference: 25-35% higher for imports

**4. Size & Availability**
Standard stock sizes (1" to 4") are cheapest. Non-standard sizes, very small bore (1/4"), and very large bore (above 12") carry premium pricing.

**5. Quantity Discount Structure**
- Under 100 kg: Standard price
- 100-500 kg: 2-3% discount
- 500 kg - 1 tonne: 3-5% discount
- 1 tonne+: Best available rate
- 5 tonne+: Special mill negotiated rate

## SS 304 vs SS 304L Price Difference

| Grade | Carbon Content | Price/Kg | Difference |
|-------|---------------|----------|-----------|
| SS 304 | 0.08% max | ₹290-310 | Base |
| SS 304L | 0.03% max | ₹295-315 | +₹5-10/kg |

SS 304L (low carbon variant) costs only ₹5-10/kg more than standard SS 304. The low carbon content prevents sensitization during welding. For welded fabrication, SS 304L is recommended and the marginal price increase is well worth it.
