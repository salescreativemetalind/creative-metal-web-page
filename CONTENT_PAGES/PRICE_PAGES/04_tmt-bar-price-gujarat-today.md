## Factors Affecting TMT Bar Price in Gujarat

**1. Iron Ore & Coal Prices**
TMT bars are made from iron ore and coking coal. International coal prices from Australia and iron ore from Odisha directly impact production costs.

**2. Season & Demand**
October to March (construction season in Gujarat) sees 5-10% higher prices due to peak demand. April-July (monsoon) typically sees softer prices and better deals.

**3. Brand Premium**
TATA Tiscon and JSW NeoSteel carry ₹2,000-3,000/tonne premium over smaller brands due to brand trust and consistent quality. SAIL offers good quality at competitive pricing.

**4. Diameter**
8mm bars are ₹3,000-4,000/tonne more expensive than 25mm bars due to higher rolling costs for smaller sections.

**5. Quantity**
Full truck load (20-25 tonnes) gets ₹500-1000/tonne better rate than small lot purchases (1-5 tonnes).

**6. GST & Transport**
18% GST applies on TMT bars. Transport from mill/stockyard adds ₹500-1500/tonne depending on distance.

## How to Check TMT Bar Quality on Site

When receiving TMT bar delivery, check these quality indicators:
1. **BIS marking:** Every bar must have ISI mark, manufacturer logo, grade marking
2. **Rib pattern:** Uniform, consistent ribs — indicates proper rolling
3. **Bend test:** 8mm bar should bend 180° without cracking
4. **Weight:** Check actual weight vs theoretical (tolerance ±5%)
5. **Surface:** No rust beyond surface oxidation, no cracks or laps
6. **Certificate:** Demand TC (Test Certificate) showing chemical and mechanical properties
