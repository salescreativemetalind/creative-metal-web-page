**Reasoning:**
1. **This page = General "SS pipe supplier" hub**
   - Covers ALL grades (304, 316L, 321, 310S, 347, 904L, Duplex, Super Duplex)
   - Covers ALL types (seamless, welded, tubes, boiler tubes)
   - Pan-India scope
   - Broad supplier intent

2. **`/ss-seamless-pipe-supplier-india` = Seamless pipe specialist page**
   - Focuses specifically on SEAMLESS pipes
   - Deep ASTM A312 standard coverage
   - Technical specifications for seamless manufacturing
   - Different search intent: users specifically want seamless pipes

3. **`/ss-304-316l-pipe-supplier-india` = Grade comparison page**
   - Focuses on PROPERTIES of 304 vs 316L
   - Material science content (composition, PREN, sensitization)
   - Comparison tables (tensile, yield, density)
   - Different search intent: users want to understand grade differences

**Differentiation Matrix:**

| Aspect | This Page | ss-seamless-pipe | ss-304-316l-pipe |
|--------|-----------|------------------|------------------|
| **Primary Focus** | General SS pipe supplier | Seamless pipe specialist | Grade 304/316L properties |

| **Depth** | Broad coverage | Deep seamless focus | Deep grade focus |
